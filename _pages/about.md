I am Jithin Rajan, a graduate student at the University of Southern California pursuing an MS in Computer Science, where I also work as a Software & Robotics Engineer at the Baum Family Maker Space.

I build software systems, some of which happen to control robot arms. At USC's Advanced Fabrication Lab I design and validate offline simulations and toolpaths for a 6-axis Stäubli TX60 and a KUKA KR100P-2, write the Python integration layer that drives them in real time, and train Vision Transformer models on live camera feeds so the robots can see what they are working on.

My interests sit at the intersection of **software engineering, computer vision and robotics**, building systems that perceive their environment and act on it, and the backend infrastructure that makes them practical. Outside the lab I work mostly on full-stack and ML projects, most recently an event-driven paper-summarization pipeline on AWS Lambda, and a local AI pipeline that turns 2D photos into 3D-printable STL meshes.

I graduated from the National Institute of Technology, Tiruchirappalli with a B.Tech. majoring in Electronics and Communication Engineering and a minor in Computer Science and Engineering. Over three years there I worked as a research assistant under the guidance of amazing professors including Dr. Hari M. Varma at IIT Bombay, Dr. Anupam Chattopadhyay at NTU Singapore, Dr. Jitin Singla at IIT Roorkee and Dr. S. Deivalakshmi at NIT Trichy.

I am currently looking for **Summer 2027 software engineering internships** — feel free to reach out by email or LinkedIn.
