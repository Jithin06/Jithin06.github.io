An event-ticketing platform backed by blockchain smart contracts. Tickets are issued and transferred on the **Tezos** chain, which makes ownership verifiable and lets transfer rules be enforced in the contract itself — capping resale price inflation by around 80% in our simulations.

The frontend is a React/Vite application with Temple Wallet integrated for authentication and wallet management.

Source and setup instructions are on [GitHub](https://github.com/Jithin06/Co-l-de-Play).
