Playing Chrome's offline dinosaur game with gestures instead of a keyboard. OpenCV tracks motion in the webcam feed, and detected gestures are translated into synthetic key presses through PyAutoGUI — a small excuse to wire a vision loop directly into a control loop.

Source and setup instructions are on [GitHub](https://github.com/Jithin06/dino_game).
