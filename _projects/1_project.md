A real-time American Sign Language interpreter: a webcam feed is passed through a hand-detection stage and a CNN classifier that maps each frame to a letter, with the recognized characters assembled into words in a Streamlit interface.

Source and setup instructions are on [GitHub](https://github.com/Jithin06/sign-language-interpreter).
